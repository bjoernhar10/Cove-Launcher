A calm, universal game launcher

Cove brings your games from Steam, GOG, Epic, Amazon, itch.io, emulators and your own folders together in one tidy library. It installs GOG, Epic and Amazon games without their launchers, runs Windows games on Linux with Proton and carries your saves between your devices. No ads, no social feed, no account of its own.

Download Cove Source code

Free and open source (MIT). Linux (x86_64, ARM64), Windows and macOS.

What Cove does

Everything runs on your computer. Logins stay in your system's keyring, and Cove never sees a password.

  • Every store in one library

    Sign in to Steam, GOG, Epic and Amazon on the store's own page. Games, playtime and achievements come along; installed games are found without any login.

  • Installs without the launchers

    GOG, Epic and Amazon games download straight through Cove, with pause, resume, repair, updates and DLC choice. itch.io and direct links too.

  • Saves on every device

    Connect your own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. Cove fetches newer saves before a game starts and uploads changed ones after playing, for every game from every store.

  • Windows games on Linux

    Proton without Steam: GE-Proton is downloaded and kept up to date, with umu-launcher, per-game prefixes, MangoHud, GameMode and gamescope.

  • Save backups

    After every session Cove backs up the game's saves. Where a game keeps them comes from an open list of over 20,000 games, inside Proton prefixes too.

  • Emulators and ROMs

    Point Cove at a ROM folder and every game shows up in your library, started with RetroArch or a standalone emulator.

  • Catalogue and wishlist

    Search and browse thousands of games, see how long they take to beat, and get a note when a game on your wishlist goes on sale.

  • TV mode and controllers

    A big-screen mode for the couch and the Steam Deck, driven by any Xbox, PlayStation, Switch or Steam controller.

  • Streaming and statistics

    Hand your games to Sunshine for Moonlight, record frame rates with MangoHud, and see where your playtime went.

Download

Every release is built on its own platform and signed for Cove's updater, which keeps Cove up to date.

Linux

AppImage, .deb and .rpm for x86_64 and ARM64, and a Flatpak bundle.

Windows

Installer (.exe) or MSI package for 64-bit Windows.

macOS

Disk image (.dmg) for Apple Silicon and Intel.
